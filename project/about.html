About Jakes Legendairy Cheese

Welcome to Jakes Legendairy Cheese! We are a family-owned business dedicated to providing the highest quality cheese products. Our commitment to excellence has made us a trusted name in the cheese industry.

How We Got Started

Founded in 2020, Jakes Legendairy Cheese began as a small family farm specializing in artisanal cheese making. What started as a passion project has grown into a beloved local business, known for our exceptional quality and commitment to traditional methods.

Who We Are

At Jakes Legendairy Cheese, we are a passionate team of cheese makers committed to delivering the finest products to our customers. Our journey is rooted in a love for the craft and a dedication to quality.

Our Values

At Jakes Legendairy Cheese, we believe in the power of quality ingredients and traditional craftsmanship. Our values include integrity, sustainability, and a deep respect for the art of cheese making.
